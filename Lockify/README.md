## Run
1. Open this folder in Android Studio (Koala or newer) and let Gradle sync finish.
2. Enable USB debugging on your phone and press Run.
3. On first launch set a PIN/pattern and **write the recovery key on paper**.
4. On the main screen turn every item to ✅ (Accessibility, Overlay, Device Admin, Battery), then switch on the apps to lock.

On Android 13+ for sideloaded apps: Settings > Apps > Lockify > ⋮ > "Allow restricted settings", then enable Accessibility again.

## Uninstall
Tap "10-min protection pause" inside the app, then uninstall within 10 minutes.
